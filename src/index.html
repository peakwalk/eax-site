Equipment and services

Upstream oil & gas equipment and services covered by EAX Marketplace

EAX Marketplace is being developed for buyers and suppliers of drilling, completion and workover equipment worldwide. The marketplace is designed around structured technical data so procurement teams can quickly find the equipment and services they require.

Representative equipment: drill bits, tubulars, centralisers, drilling string and BHA tools, casing running equipment, completion equipment, workover equipment, cementing equipment, drilling fluids, well control equipment, surface equipment, OCTG, valves, wellheads, pumps, pressure control equipment and offshore containers.

Representative services: drilling services, directional drilling, geosteering, mud engineering, cementing, wireline, LWD, MWD, mud logging, coring, well testing, completion, intervention, coiled tubing, slickline, fishing, milling, inspection, logistics, rental services and asset integrity.
