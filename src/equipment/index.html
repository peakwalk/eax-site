How buyers use EAX equipment categories

Oilfield buyers often start with an urgent operational need rather than a single part number. EAX equipment categories are designed to help procurement teams move from broad requirements into structured equipment records that can be compared quickly.

Useful listing details include equipment type, size, connection, pressure rating, material grade, condition, location, certification, inspection history, available quantity and rental or sale availability.

Clear category structure is especially useful when equipment is needed across multiple vendors, yards or regions. Buyers can evaluate whether an item is suitable for immediate mobilisation, longer-term rental, surplus purchase or future project planning. It also helps suppliers present comparable alternatives when exact specifications are not available.

What suppliers can prepare before launch

Founding suppliers can prepare inventory in the format they already use today. EAX can review spreadsheets, identify missing technical fields and help structure listings for clearer buyer discovery.

Manufacturers, dealers, rental companies, traders and liquidators can all benefit from consistent equipment records. Better listing data helps reduce back-and-forth enquiries and makes it easier for qualified buyers to understand availability before they contact the supplier. This is especially important for high-value or technically specific oilfield assets.

  • New, used, rental and surplus equipment inventory
  • Technical specifications, photos and certifications
  • Yard location, availability and preferred enquiry contact
