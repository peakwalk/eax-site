Equipment categories

Categories are organised around the way oilfield buyers search, compare and source equipment for drilling, completion, workover and field operations.

Tubulars and OCTG

Casing, tubing, drill pipe, heavy weight drill pipe, drill collars, pup joints, crossovers and related tubular products.

Surface and Rig Equipment

Rental and Surplus Equipment

Rental fleets, surplus stock, used oilfield equipment, offshore containers and equipment available for short-term or long-term projects.
